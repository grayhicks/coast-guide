Live music beside the Gulf every Tuesday evening, free, with the sand right there. Bring chairs or a blanket, grab dinner on the boardwalk, let the kids run while the band plays. 8/11 is The New Cahoots.

Before you go

  • Chairs or a blanket. There is no seating
  • Free beach trolley beats the Casino Beach parking fight
  • No pets and no glass in the concert area
  • Bug spray. 7 PM by the water
