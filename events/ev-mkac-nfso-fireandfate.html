The Northwest Florida Symphony Orchestra opens its 40th anniversary season with pianist Stewart Goodyear performing Beethoven's Piano Concerto No. 4, alongside Hailstork's Fanfare on Amazing Grace and Tchaikovsky's Symphony No. 5. The concert runs a single evening at the Mattie Kelly Arts Center.

Before you go

  • Buy tickets in advance: $35 adults, $16 for youth 18 and under.
  • Arrive at the Mattie Kelly Arts Center by 7 PM.
  • Ask at the box office about any published military or veteran rate before you buy.
