Gulf Breeze Elementary School's PTA holds its second annual fall festival on the school grounds for three hours on a Sunday afternoon, with CoWork Annex Gulf Breeze as presenting sponsor. Proceeds go toward students, teachers, school programs and enrichment. Tickets are sold through the PTA's Zeffy page, though that page does not publish a dollar amount.

Before you go

  • Check the PTA's Zeffy page for current ticket pricing before you go.
