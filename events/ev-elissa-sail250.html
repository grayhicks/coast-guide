A National Historic Landmark square-rigged iron barque, and one of only three ships of her kind left in the world that still actively sails. Pensacola is the FIRST stop on her Sail250 voyage for the nation's 250th — and only her third recorded visit ever, after 1886 and 2018. Walking her deck costs nothing. A ship-and-history kid gets rigging and 149 years of iron; the stroller rolls the pier.

Before you go

  • Park downtown — Palafox garage on Jefferson, one block over, is the reliable one
  • Walk to Palafox Pier, 895 S Palafox
  • Deck tours are self-guided — no ticket, no RSVP
  • Confirm the day's hours on galveston250.com the morning of

What we checked, and when

Read off the venue's own page on 2026-08-05.

Free self-guided deck tours, confirmed on the Galveston Historical Foundation's own Sail250 port page. The ticketed add-ons are separate and steep — Salute to Sunset Party 8/8 7–9 PM is $250/person and the Friday Day Sail is $350; neither is priced for a family of five and neither is required to board.

Not confirmed: A military rate. We searched and the venue publishes none — that is not the same as there being none, so ask.

⚠️ DECK-TOUR HOURS ARE SECONDARY-SOURCED. Reported as Fri 9 AM–noon, Sat 11 AM–5 PM, Sun 11 AM–5 PM, but the operator's own page does not publish the per-day breakdown. Check galveston250.com before you drive.

⚠️ STALE-PRESS TRAP: a May 2026 news article gives this visit as May 23–24 with different hours. That was the earlier schedule. The current visit is Aug 7–9 — do not trust the May piece.

The USCG EAGLE is also due at the pier this weekend — a second tall ship, worth asking the crew about.

Where this came from

  1. Galveston Historical Foundation — official Sail250 Pensacola port page (free self-guided deck tours, Palafox Pier, 895 South Palafox St)
  2. Must Do Pensacola — per-day deck tour hours and ticketed add-on prices
  3. Santa Rosa Press Gazette — Aug 7–9 dates, third visit after 1886 and 2018
