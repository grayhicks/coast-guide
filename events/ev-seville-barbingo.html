A free-to-play bingo night in Apple Annie's courtyard room, inside the 1871 warehouse block that has been Pensacola's night-out address for fifty years. Low stakes, no ticket, and you can leave after a card if it is not landing.

Before you go

  • Nothing to reserve — walk in
  • Apple Annie's, not the Phogg's side
  • Kitchen is open; the bingo is the free part

What we checked, and when

Read off the venue's own page on 2026-08-08.

Seville Quarter's own event page says "Bingo at 8:00 PM in Apple Annie's | Free to Play!" The bingo costs nothing. Food and drink are the venue's normal menu prices and are the whole cost of the night.

This is a bar room. No published age rule either way — Seville Quarter runs an 18+ College Night and a family-welcome Friday happy hour, so there is no blanket 21+ policy to quote. Call (850) 434-6211 if you need certainty.
