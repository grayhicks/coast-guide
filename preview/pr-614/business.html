SponsorshipWhat the money actually buys

The app is drawn, and the drawing is where a sponsor lives: the banner behind the plane, the parasail, the sign on the pier. Every picture below is the real app with a made-up business, Halyard & Co., in the spot, so you see exactly what you would be buying.

Every size below is measured on a 390 pixel phone, which is what most people are holding.

Your own card again, dressed in gold on its own band, at random spots in the lists, and one sponsor always opens the list. Your free listing stays exactly where it is.

One sponsor per category

We take one sponsor in each category at a time: one charter, one date-night restaurant, one bounce park. While yours runs, nobody in your category buys the space beside you. Your competitors stay on the list; they just can’t buy the placements next to you. If your category is taken, we tell you, and you’re first in line when it opens.

The part most people don’t put in writingWhat it never buys

  • It does not buy a position. Paying changes nothing on the list or its order. A placement is added beside it, never into it.
  • It does not buy a better write-up. Your card says what we verified, whether you are a sponsor or not.
  • It does not buy a competitor’s removal. Nobody can pay to take another business off this list. Not for any amount.
  • It does not buy silence. If we find your hours changed or your price moved, we correct the card. Being a sponsor does not freeze it.
  • It does not hide. A paid placement carries the word Advertisement or Sponsored, in that word, on the placement itself, every time.

How a placement is countedWe will tell you what we actually know

No ad server, and nothing here follows anybody around. So we cannot invent an impression count and we are not going to quote you one.

What we can tell you honestly, every month:

  • The dates your placement ran, and how many builds it shipped in. The guide rebuilds several times a day, so that is a real, checkable number.
  • Taps on your link. Your link runs through a counting redirect, so each tap is counted and the monthly total goes in your report. It counts taps, never who tapped.
  • A link you can measure yourself. We will tag it however your analytics want it, so the number is your own.
