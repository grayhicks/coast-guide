Sinfonia Gulf Coast plays the world premiere of Howard Levy's Eastern European Harmonica and Piano Concerto, with Levy himself as the soloist, followed by Beethoven's Eighth Symphony. Levy is a two-time Grammy winner, and the student ticket makes this an easy outing for a music-minded teenager. The street address of the high school is not on the ticket page.

Before you go
