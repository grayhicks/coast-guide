This spot offers the cheapest real fishing in the radius. A veteran fishes for $6.50 (military rate), kids under 6 are free, and observers pay $2.25, with pelicans, rays and dolphins included. Rod rentals are available on site.

Before you go

  • Rent one rod, share turns
  • Hold hats. It's windy
  • Dolphins usually show by mid-morning
