Destin's go-kart-and-mini-golf FEC has bumper cars, a spinning coaster, kiddie rides, and the Fat Daddy's arcade next door on the same point cards. There is something for every age in one stop, and points scale to the crew, but confirm the day's seasonal hours before you drive.

Before you go

  • Confirm today's seasonal hours by phone
  • Bigger point cards = better per-ride value for the crew
  • Height-check the karts/coaster for the little two

What we checked, and when

Read off the venue's own page on 2026-09-02.

The Track Destin has no separate admission fee; guests buy a shared point card at $1 per point, and any family member can use points off the same card, with a 60-point package at $55, 125 points at $110 and 250 points at $215, saving $5, $15 and $35 against buying points one at a time. Arcade games average one to two points each. Attractions run 5 to 27 points each; for example Family Track is 9 points and the SkyFlyer is 27 points per person. Because the point card is shared and how many points a visit needs varies widely, the total below is a two-adult subtotal using one 60-point package ($55) as a representative starting point, not a fixed family price. A five-person family doing several rides each should expect to buy more than one package. No age-based discount is published, and the Kiddie Karts and Hurricane 360 were listed as temporarily closed at last check.

⚠️ Seasonal hours. Call 850-654-4668
