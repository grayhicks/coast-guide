The big Destin water park brings slides, a lazy river and a wave pool. Buy online in advance, and it's far cheaper than the gate.

Before you go

  • Buy online in advance to halve the price
  • Confirm whether toddlers need a ticket

What we checked

This one predates the check-date stamp, so we cannot tell you the day. Everything below is what its sources say.

Big Kahuna's standard single-day, in-park walk-up admission is $64.99 per person. No child or toddler discount is published on the tickets page reviewed, so the total below assumes all five family members pay full walk-up admission (5 x $64.99 = $324.95). Buying online at least a day ahead drops the per-person price to $44.99, or $59.99 for same-day online, both real savings over walking up. Parking is separate and not included above: standard weekday parking runs $10 online or $20 in-park, and weekend and VIP tiers run higher.

Seasonal (~Apr-Sept). Verify the day's hours

⚠️ Big Kahuna's advertised military/first-responder discount prices out higher than the park's own online advance rate. Book online ahead instead of using the gate discount.
