What this isA list you can actually trust

Most local event lists are a scrape of a scrape. Dates roll over from last year, prices are whatever someone typed in 2023, and half the venues closed.

We check. Every price, every date, every set of hours gets read off the venue’s own page before it ships, and each card shows the date it was last verified. Where we could not confirm something, the card says so instead of guessing — because a wrong price is worse than no price.

✓ Priced for your family

What an adult pays, what a kid pays, at what ages, what’s free, and the senior, student and military rates when a venue publishes them. Not one generic number.

✓ Military and veteran rates

Every venue gets its own discount search, every sweep. If a rate exists we find it. If it doesn’t, we say “not published” rather than imply one.

✓ Works with no signal

Add it to your home screen and it opens on the beach, in the car, anywhere. No account, no tracking, no analytics — nothing about your plans leaves your phone.

✗ Nobody buys their way on

A business can sponsor, and when it does the card says Sponsored, in that word, every time. What money never buys is a place on the honest list.

Why we built itNo family should miss a good day because nobody told them

We’re a family on this coast. We got tired of finding out on Sunday night about the free thing that happened Saturday morning, and tired of driving forty minutes on a price that turned out to be wrong.

Everyone belongs. The dad who needs to know there’s a quiet room. The grandmother who wants the senior rate. The wheelchair user who’s tired of finding out about the stairs after they’ve already parked. Autism is personal in this house, so every plan is written plain: real time, real price, no surprises.
