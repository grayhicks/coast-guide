Terms of use

Using this site

This site is free for families to use, and always will be. Browse it, screenshot it, text a card to a friend, plan your whole weekend from it. That is what it is for.

What we ask in return

Do not scrape it. Automated collection — bots, crawlers, headless browsers, scripts, or bulk extraction of any kind — is not permitted without written permission.

Do not republish the list. You are welcome to reference individual events, and to tell people where you found them. You may not copy this listing, in whole or in substantial part, and publish it as your own — including as a feed, a newsletter, an app, a dataset, or training data for a model.

Do not resell access. This list is free. It stays free. It is not yours to charge for.

Building something that uses our data?

Ask. We are genuinely open to it, and we would rather say yes on the record than find out later. Get in touch.

What we promise

Every price, date, and hour on this site was checked against the venue's own page, and each card shows the date it was last verified. We do not charge venues to be listed, and we never leave a real event off the list because someone did not pay. Where we could not confirm a price, the card says so instead of guessing.

What we cannot promise

Venues change hours, cancel events, and raise prices without telling anyone. Confirm before you drive. We are not responsible for a closed door, and nothing here is a substitute for your own judgment about weather, safety, or what is right for your kids.

Copyright

The individual facts on this site — dates, times, addresses, prices — are facts, and facts belong to everyone. The way we have written, selected, organized, and verified them is our work, and that is what these terms protect.
