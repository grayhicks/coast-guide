          New in the designer

          • Tap words that are already picked to type new ones right on the card.
          • On a computer: Enter types, Esc picks the box around it, Tab goes to the next piece, Ctrl+B bolds, Delete hides. The ? button lists them all.
          • More, then Poster tiles, lets you style the new poster view.
          • Double-tap the picked words to type right on the card. Enter keeps them.
          • On a phone, two fingers on the picked piece make it bigger or smaller and turn it.
          • The pink dot above the picked piece turns it. Layers can bring a piece forward or send it back.
          • Hide tools (the arrow beside Reset this) gives the app the whole phone screen.
          • Drag the round handle on the picked piece to make it bigger or smaller.
          • Hold the half-filled square at the top to see the app without your changes.
          • The magnifier on the app zooms 2x or 3x on the picked piece for exact placing.
          • In Move, Even above and below lines a piece up in one tap. Dragging snaps to the words around it.
          • The + button picks more pieces, so one change goes on all of them.
          • Copy a look from one piece and paste it on another.
          • Colour has an eyedropper, colour codes, see-through, gradients and a check that it still reads.
          • Tap the hero to change its boat, flag, moon and the rest: size, turn and colour.
          • History keeps every Save, and Bring back loads an older one.
          • After Save + publish, the top of the screen says when your changes are live.
