For local business

A free listing either way, and a sponsorship that says what it is

Your listing is free and it always will be. We don’t charge for inclusion, we don’t rank paying venues higher, and we’ll never leave a real family event off the list because nobody paid us.

What we doWe verify against your own page

We read your site, not an aggregator

Prices, hours and dates come off your own page. If your page and a listing site disagree, your page wins.

We show when we last checked

Every card carries the date its facts were verified, so families can see how fresh it is.

We ask about your military rate

We run a separate search for it on every venue, every sweep. This coast is full of military families and it is the first thing they look for.

We say when we don’t know

If we can’t confirm a price we print that instead of a number. Nobody drives out on a figure we made up.

The fastest way inSend us your calendar

A scraper can only take what is already published. Your October lineup, sent to us in August, is something families cannot find anywhere else yet, and it is the hardest thing for us to find on our own.

SponsorshipWhat the money actually buys

The guide is drawn. There is a coastline across the top of every page with a boat on the water, a buoy, a cooler on the sand and a flag on its pole, and those are the places a sponsor lives. A beach is somewhere advertising already belongs, so a placement here is part of the picture rather than a banner stapled over it.

Each surface is a different size, and the sizes below are measured on a 390 pixel phone. These are measured on a 390 pixel phone, which is what most people are holding.

The small props

The cooler, the buoy, the flagpole, the name on the boat’s transom. Roughly 20 to 90 pixels across, so each one holds a logo or a single short word.

The hull

86 by 31 pixels. A painted boat carries your mark or one wordmark. We measured this before we sold it, which is why we are not promising you a sentence on the side of a boat.

The float and the banner plane

The boat can tow something, and a plane can cross the sky pulling a banner. These run the width of the water, so they are the surfaces that hold a real line of copy: an offer, a price, a weekend.

The card placement

The widest thing we have, about 346 pixels, and it sits in the list itself. Room for a mark, a line of copy and a link. It appears once every six cards and never on top of another business’s listing.

Two of those surfaces sit inside the list on the phone, and they are deliberately different sizes because they answer different budgets. Both carry the word every time.

The shelf, on every sixth card

A strip one card wide beneath a listing, 346 by 68 pixels, where a drawn creature carries your sign across it. Room for a name or a short line. It lands on every sixth card, which is the same cadence the section below runs on, so choosing between them is a question of how much room you need rather than how often you are seen. While nobody has bought it the strip is drawn with a dashed edge and carries no word at all.

The section, every sixth card

The full column, ruled above and below, with room for your name, your address, your phone and a line of copy, and a scene drawn to carry it. It reads Sponsored when it is bought and says the space is for sale when it is not. It is a different shape from a card on purpose, so nobody mistakes it for one.

Founding rateYour founding rate is held for your first twelve paid months

We’re opening a limited number of founding placements, and the rate you start at is the rate you keep for a full twelve months, no matter what the audience grows to by then.

This guide is new, and we’d rather earn that first year than price it off a guess. Ask what it costs, and we’ll tell you exactly what you get for it.

Talk to us

Creators and local businesses: a name and a link needs no artwork and can go up the day we speak. If your placement needs illustrating, we draw it in the guide’s own style, and there is no extra charge for that while founding rates are open.

The part most people don’t put in writingWhat it never buys

Every rule below is one we hold to, and you are welcome to hold us to it:

  • It does not buy a position. Paying changes nothing about what is on the list or the order it appears in. A sponsored placement is added beside the list, never inserted into it and never in place of anything.
  • It does not buy a better write-up. Your card says what we verified, whether you are a sponsor or not.
  • It does not buy a competitor’s removal. Nobody can pay to take another business off this list. Not for any amount.
  • It does not buy silence. If we find your hours changed or your price moved, we correct the card. Being a sponsor does not freeze it.
  • It does not hide. A paid placement carries the word Advertisement or Sponsored, in that word, on the placement itself, every time.

How a placement is countedWe will tell you what we actually know

This site and its app are built as static files. There is no ad server and nothing here follows anybody around, which means we cannot invent an impression count and we are not going to quote you one.

What we can tell you honestly, every month:

  • The dates your placement ran, and how many builds it shipped in. The guide rebuilds several times a day, so a share of the builds is a real, checkable thing.
  • Taps on your link. Not yet. The counting redirect is built next, and until it exists we will not claim a number we cannot produce. What you get in the meantime is the dates your placement ran and the builds it shipped in, which we can show you from the record.
  • A link you can measure yourself. If you use analytics, we will tag your link however you want it tagged, so the number you see is your own.

Once the counting redirect is built, the monthly note will carry taps as well as build counts.
