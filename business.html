For local business

Listing is free. It stays free.

We don’t charge for inclusion, we don’t rank paying venues higher, and we’ll never leave a real family event off the list because nobody paid us. That is not a launch promotion. It is the product.

What we doWe verify against your own page

We read your site, not an aggregator

Prices, hours and dates come off your own page. If your page and a listing site disagree, your page wins.

We show when we last checked

Every card carries the date its facts were verified. Families can see how fresh it is instead of guessing.

We ask about your military rate

Every venue, every sweep, its own search. This coast is full of military families and it is the first thing they look for.

We say when we don’t know

If we can’t confirm a price we print that instead of a number. Nobody drives out on a figure we made up.

The fastest way inSend us your calendar

A scraper can only take what is already published. Your October lineup, sent to us in August, is something families cannot find anywhere else yet. That is the single most useful thing you can hand us.

SponsorshipComing, and it will be labelled

We are building a small local sponsorship program so this stays free for families. It is not open yet and there is nothing to buy on this site today.

When it opens, two things will be true and stay true: a sponsored card will say Sponsored, in that word, every time, and sponsorship will never change what is on the honest list or what order it appears in.

If you want to hear about it first, say so on the submission form and we’ll come to you.
